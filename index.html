How It Works

A simple, transparent, and structured workflow from contract creation to final payment

01

Create Project

The client outlines the project scope, overall timeline, requirements, and total budget.

02

Define Milestones

Split the project into logical phases. Attach specific deliverables and allocate funds to each phase.

03

Submit & Review

The freelancer submits completed work for a milestone. The client reviews and provides direct feedback.

04

Approve & Track

Upon approval, the milestone is marked complete, and the simulated payment status is updated to released.
